A space dedicated to maturity, change, and the wisdom carried through lived experience. We explore ageing, menopause, sensuality, and our relationship with endings and transitions — with an invitation to meet the changing body with curiosity, acceptance, and respect.

Individual bodywork and guidance for men who want to better understand their bodies, sensitivity, intimacy and physical responses. A space to explore specific challenges, or simply develop greater body awareness, confidence and connection.

01 · Areas of Support

Different bodies,
different needs.

You do not need to identify with a specific problem before booking. The work can also support curiosity, greater awareness and a deeper understanding of your own body.

Erectile difficulties

Working with tension, breath and attention rather than performance.

Premature ejaculation

Understanding arousal patterns, regulation and body signals.

Reduced sensitivity or experience

A slow return to embodied sensation and presence.

Tension or pain during intimacy

Attentive bodywork with pelvic tension and areas of discomfort.

Consequences of surgeries or injuries

Focused work on scar tissue, mobility and comfort.

Disconnection from the body

Rebuilding awareness of what you feel and how you respond.

Excessive pornography consumption

Understanding habits, expectations and embodied intimacy.

Understanding the female body

A grounded, respectful space to learn and ask openly.

The work is not about achieving a specific sexual outcome. It is about understanding body signals, tension, sensitivity, arousal patterns and boundaries — and your own individual experience.
